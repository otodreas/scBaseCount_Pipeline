01 · Landscape

What came before scBaseCount

Three public models. None of them ingest SRA as a uniformly processed 10X store. scBaseCount still has a job across all three.

GEO

Loose schemas

Depositors choose how much metadata to send. Many records are thin as sample context. Missing cell_type often starts here.

CELLxGENE

Community-contributed

Standardized cell-level metadata on submission. Strong for exploration. Not a reprocessing of SRA as a whole.

HCA / HLCA

Lab-harmonized

Selected studies, consensus cell-type maps. The Human Lung Cell Atlas is curated, not exhaustive of SRA.

scBaseCount

Agent-curated, uniform matrices

AI discovery and metadata extraction, one STAR reference, one h5ad per SRX. No clusters. No tissue cut. No batch correction.

Clough & Barrett 2016; CZI Cell Science Program 2025; Rozenblatt-Rosen et al. 2017; Sikkema et al. 2023; Youngblut et al. 2025. Report Introduction.

01 · Landscape

Part of scBaseCount already passed through CELLxGENE

Say this now: it is why some files have cell_type, why others do not, and why we cut the store to lung.

Labels and holes

CXG in, GEO often empty

Portal submissions carry cell-level labels. Those become our clustering prior and the key we use to ask whether Harmony erased biology. GEO-style records often never had that field. A file with no usable cell_type never enters the atlas: 238 files, 1,509,128 barcodes (Table 1).

Why lung

2.6M cases · 1.9M deaths (2024)

Lung cancer was estimated the most frequent cancer and the leading cause of cancer death. IPF sits in the same tissue with high mortality. The construction problem is still: pick the tissue, share a gene axis, integrate without erasing cell types.

CZI Cell Science Program 2025; Sung et al. 2026; Chakraborty et al. 2022. Report Introduction and Table 1.

04 · Limits

Atlases are not general-purpose

Batch effects force a new sample cut, and often a new correction, for each task. This object is a lung starting point, not an answer key.

Hundreds of GB
cloud storage to hold the matrices
Order of TB
memory: concat keeps the atlas in RAM until write-out
  • Donor, disease, and grade can still sit inside BioProject. A disease contrast should not pretend those are gone.
  • Agent metadata can disagree with BioSample. Re-check the tube for any label-dependent question.
  • No wall-clock runtimes in the report. Doublets and HVG method were not prioritized.

Luecken et al. 2022; Mueller et al. 2026. Report Discussion. The atlas can support cluster-level disease questions; those contrasts are not this talk.

Takeaways

Where we are

  • GEO, CELLxGENE, and HCA each solve a different archive problem. scBaseCount still supplies uniform 10X matrices at SRA scale, including agent holes.
  • Largest eligible-set cell loss: gene-count floor (1,600,056). Inside kept files: mitochondrial fraction.
  • Resolution calibrated on five files, then 100k, then the atlas: Leiden 0.8, 71 clusters.
  • Harmony on BioProject: scIB batch 0.28 → 0.45, bio 0.53 → 0.51.
  • The atlas is not general-purpose. Re-harmonize for the next task.

Youngblut et al. 2025; Luecken & Theis 2019; Luecken et al. 2022; Korsunsky et al. 2019. Report Table 1, Fig 1–3.
